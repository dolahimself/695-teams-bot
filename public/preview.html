Bear Friends Stories

A quick way to share a story from camp, family support, youth programs or community events with the CFSGE Foundation team.

How it works

  1. Pick the program and answer two short questions.
  2. Check your story on the review card.
  3. Tick the box to confirm you have permission to share it, then tap Submit. Nothing is sent until you do.

Before you submit, check

Ontario law protects personal information about the services children and families receive, and the agency must keep it safe and secure. Read down this list once before you tap Submit.

  • No names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, links, or school or place names.
  • No health, disability, financial, court or police details about the child or family.
  • The person the story is about, or the person who can decide for them, has agreed to share it. For a child, a parent or guardian may need to agree.
  • They know they can say no, and can change their mind later.
  • Any photo shows no faces, names or school names.
  • The story uses respectful, dignity-centred language.
  • You are sharing it in this chat, not by text, email or voicemail. The agency's safeguards cover those too.

Photos

Photos only, such as reading notes or drawings. Make them anonymous first: no faces, names or school names. Attach them in the chat before you tap Submit and they go with your story.

What the bot checks

The bot looks for the obvious, like emails, phone numbers, links and postal codes, and asks you to remove them. It is a light check and does not catch everything, so please read your story once before you submit.

What happens next

Stories go to a private list. The Foundation team reviews each one, and nothing is used until it is approved.

If something went wrong

If you shared something you should not have, or a story should not be used, contact the agency's Privacy Lead on 1-888-753-8681.
