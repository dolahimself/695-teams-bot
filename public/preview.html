Bear Friends Desk

A quick way to share a story from camp, family support, youth programs or community events with the CFSGE Foundation team.

How it works

  1. Pick the program and answer two short questions.
  2. Check your story on the review card.
  3. Tap Send for review. Nothing is sent until you do.

Photos, audio and video

Attach a file in the chat before you tap Send for review and it goes with your story.

Privacy

Please leave out names, phone numbers, emails, addresses and links. The bot checks for these and asks you to remove them.

Need help?

Type help in the chat, or start over to begin again.
